Flack

Your messages, riding shotgun.

Hear Slack direct messages in Android Auto and answer them by voice. Flack hands your reply to the Slack app on your phone, which sends it.

Download flack.apk Install steps

For Android 12 or later. Open the download on your phone.

How it works

  1. Flack reads notifications from the official Slack app only, and ignores every other app.
  2. While Android Auto is connected, a new direct message appears in the car, ready to read aloud.
  3. Reply by voice and confirm. Flack passes your words to Slack's reply button once. If it cannot tell where a reply would go, it offers none.

Install

  1. Download flack.apk on your phone and open it. Allow your browser to install apps if Android asks.
  2. Open Flack and follow its five setup steps. If notification access is greyed out, the app shows how to allow it.
  3. In Android Auto's developer settings, turn on Unknown sources. Flack is not from Google Play, so Android Auto hides it until you do.

The full install guide has each step in detail.

Privacy

  • Flack has no internet permission, so it cannot send your messages anywhere.
  • It has no Slack login or token. The Slack app does the sending.
  • Message text stays in memory. It is never saved, backed up or logged.
  • The only permission it asks for is posting notifications.

Status

Flack is an experiment for one-to-one direct messages, built for two testers. It has not been tested in a car yet. Group DMs, channels and threads stay off until each one is tested on a real phone. Results go in the device test record.

Flack is an independent project. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Slack, Google or Samsung.
